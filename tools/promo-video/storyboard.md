## Continuity and audio

Every cut uses a consistent left-to-right mask wipe with a warm edge. Staggered reveals make the narrative sequential. Browser crops preserve their source aspect ratio. Captions stay below the content safe area. Chapter numbering and a fine progress line provide continuity.

The original generated score is 92 BPM in A minor, using softly voiced chords, a restrained arpeggio, bass, gentle percussion, transition swishes and two-note UI confirmations. It begins and ends with fades. The encode normalizes loudness to -18 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling target.

## Demo editorial note

The fictional transcript is “Finish the landing page and review the Vercel deployment”. A terminal full stop is deliberately omitted because the current extractor's simple `and` regular expression handles the unpunctuated sample as two tasks. No application logic was modified. The film preserves the second task's actual lowercase “review” rather than inventing text normalization.

The sync capture returns a deliberate authorization failure in the isolated browser, preserving the real failure/retry UI. No production Notion write was attempted. The successful architecture illustration is explanatory and explicitly marked as such.
