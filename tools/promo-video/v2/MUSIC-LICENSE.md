# V2 music and sound design

Title: **Capture at speed**

An original instrumental generated by `audio.mjs` for this Hazlo production: 120 BPM, two-step / minimal-house-inspired drum programming, extended-chord stabs, bass, percussion and editorial Foley.

No third-party track, recording, sample pack, scraped audio, copyrighted trending song, paid API, or music subscription is used. The synthesizer, seeded noise sources, instrument envelopes, arrangement, effects and generated audio are included as reproducible source and may be used and redistributed under the Hazlo repository's MIT license.

There are no separate music attribution requirements. Preserve the repository's existing MIT license and copyright notice when redistributing source. Third-party software tools and system fonts retain their own licenses; their binaries are not copied into the media assets.

The music and sound-effects stems are generated separately as `assets/v2/audio/music.wav` and `effects.wav`. `mix.wav` is the original combined render input. These generated WAV files are ignored by Git and can be regenerated with `pnpm v2:audio`.

The output has no voiceover. English scene captions describe the visible story rather than claiming unrecorded spoken narration.
