## Palette and typography

Ivory `#f7f5f0`, charcoal `#111316`, slate `#242832`, ink `#18212f`, gold `#ffd98a` and muted gray derive from the current UI. Gold is reserved for a few headlines and active editorial traces. Green appears only where relevant in genuine task completion/success semantics; no green lighting is used. Bahnschrift headlines, Segoe UI body and Consolas configuration identifiers are system fonts.

## Transitions

The hook/product and capture/tasks joins use short upward panel moves. Product/capture is a UI match cut. The voice-to-typing join reveals the light surface with a short panel mask. Tasks/sync and sync/setup use opposite-direction pushes. The close starts with a horizontal aperture mask that avoids ghosted text, then its own typography reveal. Panel transitions last approximately 0.28 seconds, with a restrained 1.4 px maximum motion blur, and carry intentional screen geometry rather than random effects.

## Audio

The new instrumental is composed and synthesized locally. A 120 BPM two-step groove combines syncopated kicks, backbeat snare/clap, shuffled hats, short extended-chord stabs, bass movement and occasional bright fills. The last two seconds reduce density for the closing mark. Transition swishes, reveal ticks and local completion pips are rendered from shared editorial times. The master is normalized to a -14 LUFS target with a -1.2 dBTP ceiling target; actual encoded measurements are recorded in validation JSON.
