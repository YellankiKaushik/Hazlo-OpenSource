- UI screenshots and the manual-entry recording show Hazlo, captured from its public deployment in a new isolated browser. Only fictional task text was entered. The application is MIT licensed by YellankiKaushik; its license and attribution remain intact.
- `assets/capture-manifest.json` records the capture target, behaviors, sample text and sync interception. No personal browser profile, cookies, request headers, credentials or Notion workspace content are included.
- The soundtrack and Foley are original procedural compositions generated by `scripts/audio.mjs`. No downloaded recording, sample library, commercial track, or third-party audio API was used. The editable audio code and resulting score may be used and redistributed under the repository's MIT license.
- Motion graphics, generic diagram symbols, waveform and typography layouts are created by the supplied source. The waveform is labelled illustrative. The Notion-style preview is explanatory, uses fictional text, and is labelled illustrative; it is not a captured private page.
- The film names third-party technologies factually. React, Vite, TypeScript, Zustand, Web Speech, Vercel and Notion remain the work and property of their respective authors. Their logos are not invented or repurposed as Hazlo ownership marks.
- Segoe UI / Arial are used from the rendering system. Font binaries are not copied or redistributed. Use appropriately licensed installed fonts when regenerating.
- The earlier promotional video is not sampled, overwritten or deleted. Its existing watermark is not used in the new film.

The maintainer-approved exports and sharing links are listed in [docs/media/README.md](../../docs/media/README.md). The production scripts themselves do not upload, commit, push or replace published media. The two published files preserve the reviewed encodes without transcoding.
