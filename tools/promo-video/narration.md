# Optional human narration

This is a recording-ready script. The delivered film uses music and on-screen text, without synthetic narration. Its SRT describes the on-screen story, not imaginary spoken dialogue. If a voiceover is recorded later, retime captions to the actual recording.

| Time | Narration |
| --- | --- |
| 00–08 | Great ideas don't wait. But stopping to capture them can break your flow. |
| 08–17 | Meet Hazlo: voice-first task capture, built by Kaushik, and open source. |
| 17–30 | Speak your thoughts using supported browser speech recognition. Or choose Type instead. Finish the landing page, and review the Vercel deployment. Capture it in one place. |
| 30–41 | Hazlo keeps your original thought and extracts useful task structure with local rules. Simple phrases become a clearer next step. |
| 41–55 | The entry saves in your browser first. A small Vercel backend can then create a page in your own Notion workspace. If sync fails, your local entry remains, ready to retry. |
| 55–65 | A small, understandable web stack connects capture, local state, and Notion. The implementation is there to inspect and adapt. |
| 65–79 | Fork the repository. Connect your own Notion integration and data source. Configure Vercel, and deploy a personal instance. Your workspace. Your setup. |
| 79–88 | Capture first. Organize later. Built for the way thoughts happen. |
| 88–95 | Try Hazlo. Open source. Forkable. Yours. |

Read naturally, approximately 135–145 words per minute. Leave the final URLs on screen; do not read them out as long strings. Avoid overstating extraction, browser support, authentication, or sync guarantees.
